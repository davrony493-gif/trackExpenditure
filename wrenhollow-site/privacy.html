Privacy notice

How Wrenhollow members' information is used.

Wrenhollow is a fictional business and this notice is a placeholder. Have it reviewed and replace the contact details before collecting real people's data.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored securely by our account provider; we never see them.
  • Booking requests you make: the experience, date and number of guests.
  • Loyalty stamps added by our team when you visit.
  • Whether you'd like marketing emails. This is off unless you tick the box, and you can change it at any time under My account.
  • Questions you type into the "Ask the brewer" chat are sent to an AI service to generate the reply. Please don't include personal details there.

Why

To run your account, handle your booking requests, keep your loyalty card, and, only if you opted in, send you news about new releases and events.

Where it's stored

Account and booking data is stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase. Each member can only access their own information.

Your choices

  • Update your name and marketing choice under My account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete your account, by emailing hello@example.com (placeholder address).

Age

Accounts, tastings and our drinks are for people aged 18 and over.
